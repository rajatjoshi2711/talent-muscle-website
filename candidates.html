Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer remote positions?

Yes. We have remote, hybrid, and on-site opportunities depending on the project and client requirements. We'll always be upfront about the work arrangement before any discussion.

What types of roles do you typically work with?

We work across the full spectrum of tech roles including developers, QA engineers, data professionals, RPA specialists, architects, and project managers. If it's tech, we likely work with it.

How long does the process take?

We move quickly. After an initial conversation, suitable opportunities are typically shared within a few days. The overall timeline depends on the client's process, but we aim to keep things moving.

Are these contract or full-time roles?

Both. We work with contract, contract-to-hire, and full-time engagements. We'll find what best fits your situation.

Can I refer someone?

Absolutely. If you know a talented engineer looking for their next role, put them in touch with us at hr@talentmuscle.com.
